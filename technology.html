TECHNOLOGY / THE FOUNDATIONS

Every large infrastructure rests on a few transversal foundations.

Two of them carry everything: the data centre, where all the information is stored, and the transport backbone — the wired and wireless network the data travels on. Cybersecurity is not a third foundation; it is a cover over them all, built into the design of every layer.

On the transversal foundations, the systems we integrate.

On top of these foundations live and travel diverse services — voice communications, information between users, security and control. Within these blocks sit all the systems we integrate, with well-defined interfaces so they work as one.

1 COMMUNICATIONS
Telephony & intercom

Critical wireless voice

2 INFORMATION
