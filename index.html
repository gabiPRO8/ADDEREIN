2 / WHO WE ARE

Engineers specialised in the core of the business.

We are a communications and control engineering company for transport and public-use infrastructure environments. Our team is made up of telecommunications engineers with broad experience and a high level of technical specialisation.

We focus on what truly sustains our clients' business: the data centre, the communications network — wired and wireless — and cybersecurity. On that transversal IT infrastructure grow all the services and systems that give the operation its functionality.

4 / OUR STORY

Born to add.

Adderein was born from a simple realisation: in large infrastructure projects, the hardest problems live at the interfaces — where many systems must meet and work as one — and that is exactly where the gaps appear. We created the company to fill those gaps: to add, from within, the engineering knowledge that acts as the glue holding every system together so the whole operation works.

Addere+In

As our name says, we exist to add value — from inside our clients' teams.
